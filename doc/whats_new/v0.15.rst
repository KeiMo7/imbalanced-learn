Enhancements
............

- Samplers now treat ``sample_weight`` as a first-class citizen. Passing
  ``sample_weight`` to ``fit_resample`` returns the resampled weights as a third
  output, aligned row-wise with the resampled ``X`` and ``y``. Original weights
  are kept for retained rows; generated rows follow the new ``weight_strategy``
  parameter (``"inherit"``, ``"nearest"``, ``"cluster"`` or a callable).
  Zero-weight rows are excluded from neighbour searches and sample generation,
  and negative, missing, mismatched or all-zero weights are rejected before any
  sampling starts. A new ``class_weight`` argument applies class-level
  multiplicative factors on top of the row weights. :pr:`TBD` by :user:`KeiMo7`.
- :class:`~imblearn.pipeline.Pipeline` propagates the resampled weights through
  its sampling steps and forwards them to the final estimator, keeping ``X``,
  ``y`` and ``sample_weight`` aligned. The unweighted path is unchanged.
  :pr:`TBD` by :user:`KeiMo7`.
