Enhancements
............

- Added :class:`imblearn.over_sampling.MultiLabelSMOTE`, an over-sampler for
  multi-label indicator targets. Samples are grouped by their full label-set
  combination and SMOTE interpolation is run inside each group, so synthetic
  samples always carry a combination observed in the training data. Both dense
  and sparse indicator matrices are supported, with the sparse path reading
  label sets directly from the CSR ``indices``/``indptr`` and never densifying
  the target. Per-label (``label_budget``) and global (``total_budget``)
  synthetic budgets, singleton combination policies (``keep``/``merge``/
  ``skip``) and an all-zero-row policy are provided. Existing single-label
  samplers are unchanged.
  By :user:`imbalanced-learn developers`.
